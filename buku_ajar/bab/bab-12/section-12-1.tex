\section{AI Sederhana: Chase dan Patrol}

\begin{learningoutcomes}
\item Mengimplementasikan enemy chase (follow player)
\item Mengimplementasikan patrol behavior
\item Menggunakan NavMesh untuk pathfinding (3D)
\end{learningoutcomes}

\subsection{Chase AI}

Cari Transform player. Di Update: direction = (player.position - transform.position).normalized. MoveTowards atau velocity. Rotate menghadap player. Simple namun efektif untuk top-down/shooter.

\subsection{Patrol AI}

Array waypoints. Current waypoint index. MoveTowards(waypoints[index]). Saat sampai: index = (index+1) \% length. Atau patrol between two points.
